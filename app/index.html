Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours, 1 playthrough

PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10 and about 200 hours: the story takes 60–80 hours, the 100% checklist about 100, and Strangers & Freaks plus cleanup about 10. One playthrough is enough; free roam continues after the credits.

  1. Stage 1 — Story, Chapters 1–2 (start here)
    Finish Gone Fishing, Chapter 1 and Chapter 2. Do Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 (see Positive Mental Attitude). Scan vehicles with WAiNK and study animals with binoculars as you go — both count toward 100% later.
  2. Stage 2 — Story, Chapters 3–5 & the final mission
    Complete Chapters 3 and 4 and the final mission for the gold. Duo free roam (from Coffee Break) unlocks the partner trophies: Hot Together and Retail Therapy. Bank-vault robberies open in Chapter 4.
  3. Stage 3 — Stranger strands & character questlines
    Finish 8 Strangers & Freaks strands (Kirk, Kyle, Yago, Pilot Pete and more) and the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret. All stay available after the story.
  4. Stage 4 — Collectibles, activities & the 100% checklist
    The big grind: 250 stunts (300 for 100%), 42 Shooting Range golds, 14 drone-race locations, 18 meditation spots, 3 card sets, 100 WAiNK locations, 50 challenges (70 for 100%), 100 vehicles, 52 areas of Leonida, 9 gangs, 8 garages, 10 Scooter Bros registrations — then A Long Road (gold) for 100% completion.
  5. Stage 5 — Cleanup & platinum
    Mop up whatever is left — the delayed pops, the money trophies ($250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent) and the 500 criminal-profile actions — and Vice Got a Price! unlocks by itself.
